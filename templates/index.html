Why Use an ATS-Friendly Resume Template?

Over 90% of Fortune 500 companies and mid-sized employers screen candidate resumes through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever. Standard resumes with complex tables, columns, or graphic icons frequently fail parsing algorithms, causing qualified candidates to get rejected before human recruiters ever see them.

Every resume template on Resumora is crafted with single-column linear parsing, standard section headers (Experience, Education, Skills), high metric density, and zero hidden formatting tags. After customizing your resume, you can export it to pure text-layer PDF or convert it using our integrated Word to PDF and PDF to Word engines.
