About Resumora Universe

Welcome to Resumora.github.io — an engineered client-side utility portal created to empower job seekers, students, and professionals with 100% free career architecture and privacy-first document processing.

100% Free & Open Access

No subscriptions, no hidden paywalls, and no credit cards required. High-resolution ATS resumes and PDF tools are freely accessible to everyone.

Privacy-First Processing

Resume editing and critical conversions run directly inside your client browser. We never store, sell, or index your personal career documents.

Our Mission

Modern job applications require precision formatting to bypass automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Workday, Taleo, and Greenhouse. However, many online tools lock basic PDF downloads behind deceptive subscription trials. Resumora was built to break that cycle by offering transparent, enterprise-quality utilities at zero cost.

Client-Side Architecture

By leveraging modern WebAssembly, Canvas rendering, and client-side JavaScript APIs, operations like photo compression (to exact limits such as 20KB or 50KB), PDF page splitting, and document combining happen instantaneously on your device without transmitting unneeded data to foreign servers.

Comprehensive Career & Document Ecosystem

Beyond our resume builder and template preview hub, Resumora provides:

  • PDF Organization: Client-side PDF Merger and Page Range Splitter.
  • Format Conversions: High-fidelity Word to PDF and PDF to Word layout translation.
  • Asset Optimization: Adaptive binary-search image compression for government portals, visa forms, and job applications.
  • Immigration Intelligence: Real-time visa guidelines and tuition estimates for students aspiring to settle in USA, Australia, Germany, and New Zealand.
