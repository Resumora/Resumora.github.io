About Resumora

Welcome to Resumora.github.io, a fast, lightweight, and intuitive tool designed to help professionals and students build polished, ATS-ready resumes effortlessly.

Our Mission

Our goal is to eliminate bloated layouts, hidden paywalls, and frustrating formatting issues. We believe clean, high-performance tools should be accessible directly in your browser without requiring complex software installations.

Client-Side Security & Speed

Resumora.github.io runs entirely client-side, meaning your personal details, career history, and sensitive data remain strictly inside your browser. We do not store or sell your personal career documents.

Continuous Improvement

We are constantly expanding our utility features—from resume export options to everyday document and image utilities—to give you a smooth, distraction-free workflow.
