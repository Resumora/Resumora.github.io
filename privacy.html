Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your work progress and draft preferences may be cached locally in your browser's local storage (LocalStorage) so that you don't lose progress upon reloading. You can clear this anytime through your browser settings.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use minimal standard analytics or performance cookies to understand web traffic patterns, page load efficiency, and usability trends. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may place cookies to serve relevant ads based on prior visits.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of outside websites.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect platform updates or legal standards. Changes will be posted directly to this page.
